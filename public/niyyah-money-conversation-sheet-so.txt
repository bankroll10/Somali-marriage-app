SHEEKADA LACAGTA
Xaashi loogu talagalay laba qof

guursadaan. Wada buuxiya ka hor inta aysan qoysasku ka wada hadlin lacagta.
Qof walba ha ku jawaabo tiirka u gaarka ah.

Afartan arrimood si gaar ah ayaa loo kala saaray. Meherka, kharashka arooska
iyo deymaha hore ee midkiin ama labadiinnaba lagu leeyahay waa arrimo kala
duwan. Lacagta bil kasta akoonnadiinna ka baxda ee aad u dirtaan dad ku nool
halkan ama dibadda iyaduna waa arrin gaar ah.
