THE MONEY CONVERSATION
A sheet for two people

A sheet for two adults considering marriage, to work through before the
families are involved. Each of you answers in your own column.

The four subjects are kept apart on purpose. Mahr is not the wedding. The
wedding is not what either of you already owes. And none of the three is the
money that leaves your account every month for people at home or abroad.
