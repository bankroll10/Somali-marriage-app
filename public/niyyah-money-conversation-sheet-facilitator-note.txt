HANDING THIS TO A COUPLE
A note for facilitators

A note for whoever gives out the money conversation sheet — a coordinator, a
counsellor, an imam, anyone running a session where it might come up. The
sheet is twenty questions across four subjects — mahr, the wedding, existing
debt, money sent to relatives — that two people fill in on their own, then
compare.


WHEN TO HAND IT OUT

- At the start of a session, before the couple talks with you — so they
  arrive having already thought it through.
- To a couple who's getting serious, to take home and come back to.
- Left in a stack at check-in. This note is the only explanation it needs.


HOW IT'S MEANT TO BE USED

- Each person fills their own column separately — not side by side. The
  point is what each of them actually thinks before the other's answer
  shapes it.
- They compare afterward, together, and mark what they agree on and what's
  still open in the box at the end of each subject.


WHAT HAPPENS TO IT AFTER

Nobody collects it. Not turned in, not read by a counsellor or coordinator,
not recorded anywhere by Niyyah or by you. It leaves with the couple.


WHICH VERSION TO USE

niyyah-money-conversation-sheet-1page.html — one page, short answer lines. A
stack at check-in, or a quick look before a longer session.

niyyah-money-conversation-sheet.html — four pages, room to actually write.
Once a couple wants to sit down with it.

Both also exist in Somali — niyyah-money-conversation-sheet-1page-so.html for
the one-page version, niyyah-money-conversation-sheet-so.html for the
four-page — for anyone in the room who reads that more comfortably, a parent
or an older relative.
